<Callout type="warn" title="Authorized testing only">
  Everything documented in this reference is intended for **authorized**
  security testing — penetration tests, bug bounty programs in scope, CTFs,
  or your own infrastructure. Hacking tools are software programs or
  utilities designed to assist in gaining access to computer systems or
  networks. Use them responsibly, legally, and only within an authorized
  scope. Running active scans, brute-force, or exploit-attempt traffic
  against systems you do not have permission to test may be illegal.
</Callout>
